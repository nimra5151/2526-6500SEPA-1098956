## Step 3: Configure Settings

1. **Settings (gear icon) → Responses tab:**
   - ✅ "Collect email addresses" — Turn OFF (so respondents can fill anonymously)
   - ✅ "Limit to 1 response" — Turn ON

2. **Theme (palette icon):**
   - Color: Indigo / Blue (matches the TutorBridge theme)
   - Font: Default

3. **Send button (top right):**
   - Select the 🔗 Link tab
   - ✅ Check "Shorten URL"
   - Copy the link → Share on WhatsApp

## Step 5: Calculate Overall Rating (after collecting responses)

Google Forms → Responses → Download as Google Sheets / Excel

### 1. SUS Score Formula (Q10–Q19):
- **Odd questions** (Q10, Q12, Q14, Q16, Q18): subtract 1 from each score
- **Even questions** (Q11, Q13, Q15, Q17, Q19): subtract score from 5
- Sum all 10 adjusted scores → multiply by **2.5**
- **SUS Score = ___ / 100**

| SUS Score | Grade | Meaning |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| 85 – 100 | A | Excellent |
| 68 – 84 | B | Good |
| 50 – 67 | C | Average |
| Below 50 | F | Poor |

### 2. Average Overall Rating (Q26):
- Add all scores → Divide by number of responses
- **Average Score = ___ / 10**

### 3. Task Completion Rate (Q4):
- Count "Completed easily" + "Completed with difficulty" per task
- Divide by total responses × 100
- **Completion Rate = ___%**

### 4. Recommendation Rate (Q27):
- Count "Yes, definitely" ÷ total responses × 100
- **Recommendation Rate = ___%**

| Overall Score | Rating |
|---------------|--------|
| 9 – 10 | Excellent |
| 7 – 8 | Good |
| 5 – 6 | Average |
| Below 5 | Needs Improvement |
